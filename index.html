Tell us about yourself

The idea

Friendship before programming.

This is intentionally simple. You are not signing up for a course. We want to help two students meet, discover what they have in common, learn from their differences, and see where the friendship goes.

Shared life

Coffee, meals, sports, music, exploring, conversation, or simply hanging out.

Mutual curiosity

Learn about SVF and NPU, Sweden and Chicago, and the experiences that shape each of you.

Faith & life

Both Christians and students open to Christian faith are welcome. Faith can be part of the conversation without being forced.
